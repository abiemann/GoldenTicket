GoldenTicket

Piece annotation workbench

Developer tool for preparing training labels. No ML model is running here. Photos and labels stay in this browser; nothing is uploaded.

PNG or JPEG, up to 48 MB each and 16,384 pixels per side. Use upright photos without EXIF rotation, private cards, or annotation outlines.

Open every matching photo first. Import replaces labels for the photos in the file after all entries pass validation. Drafts may have unfinished groups or reviews.

Labels are held in memory. Export before closing; there is no autosave.
